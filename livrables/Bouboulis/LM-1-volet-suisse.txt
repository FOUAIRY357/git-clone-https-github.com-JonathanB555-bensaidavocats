IT IS FIRST RECALLED THAT:

The Lawyer and the Client have discussed together the nature of the mission entrusted to the Lawyer under this agreement (hereinafter, "the Agreement"), as well as the various methods of remuneration available under the law and professional practice.
Under the Agreement, the parties agree to define the mission and the method of remuneration of the Lawyer.

1. Mission
The Client instructs the Lawyer to establish and pursue the recovery of the sums due to the estate of the late Krassimira Bouboulis Borozanova in connection with the Valais succession-tax line, and to determine the effect of the settlement agreement of 12 February 2025 upon that line.

More specifically, the Lawyer is to:
- give a reasoned position on each of the nine items raised in the Client's memorandum of 26 August 2026, and identify those which may properly be maintained
- establish the fate of the balance of CHF 302,870 notified by the Canton of Valais in favour of the estate, and whether it has been reimbursed, to whom and on what date
- establish whether the corresponding claim was carried on the assets side of the inventory of 30 June 2024, in which the advance of CHF 436,495 appears as a deduction in full, and quantify the consequences for the division of the estate
- determine the applicable time limits, and the Client's standing to act alone or jointly with the other heirs, in the estate of the late Krassimira Bouboulis Borozanova and in that of the late Borislav Borozanov
- prepare and send the requests for production to the Cantonal Tax Administration in Sion, to Lombard Odier and to the executor, then handle the replies and the follow-up
- advise the Client on the steps to be taken in the light of the replies obtained

The Lawyer undertakes to carry out all due diligence and to implement all legal and procedural means to protect the Client's interests and to give them the best chances of success. The Lawyer will carry out all useful diligence in agreement with the Client. The Lawyer will keep the Client regularly informed of the progress of the mission entrusted to him.

2. Determination of the fee
Fixed monthly fee
For the performance of the mission, the fees are set on a fixed basis at the sum of EUR 7,500 excluding VAT per month (seven thousand five hundred euros excluding tax per month). Amounts are stated excluding tax; VAT is due in addition, according to the regime applicable at the date of invoicing.
For information, the firm's usual hourly rate is set at EUR 600.00 excluding VAT, 2026 value.

These fees cover all diligence carried out within the scope of the mission: study of the file in the light of the documents provided by the Client and of the applicable legislation and case law, correspondence with the Canton of Valais, with the bank and with the executor, and the drafting and finalisation of the written work product.

The Agreement is entered into for an indefinite period and may be terminated by either party at the end of any month, by written notice given before the last day of that month. Fees already paid for a month begun remain acquired to the Lawyer.

At the end of the second month, the parties will review together the scope of the mission and the amount of the fee in the light of the results obtained.

The fee does not cover: any contentious or judicial proceedings, in Switzerland or elsewhere, which will be the subject of a separate agreement; the fees of local correspondents, in particular in Switzerland and in Bulgaria; and disbursements, in particular the European Certificate of Succession, certificates of inheritance, apostilles, sworn translations and administrative charges, which are invoiced to the Client at cost.

Contingency (result) fee
In addition to the monthly fee above, a result fee of 10% excluding VAT will be due to the Lawyer, calculated on the sums actually recovered in connection with the Valais succession-tax line, whether recovered from the Canton of Valais, from the estate or from any other party, and whether by way of payment, of set-off, or of an increase in the Client's share upon a division of the estate. Where the sums recovered are denominated in a currency other than the euro, they are converted at the European Central Bank reference rate applicable on the date of receipt.
This result fee will fall due upon the occurrence of the following trigger event: the effective receipt of those sums by the Client, or their crediting to an account of the estate in which the Client holds a share. It will remain due to the Lawyer in full once that event occurs, including after the end or the interruption of the mission, and regardless of which lawyer finalised the file, notwithstanding any withdrawal of the mission or any succession of lawyers.

3. Payment
The monthly fee is payable in advance. The first month is payable upon signature of the Agreement, and each subsequent month on the first day of the month concerned. Invoices for costs and fees are payable on receipt. Failing payment when due, late-payment interest will be legally due, calculated at a rate equal to three times the legal interest rate as from the due date shown on the invoice, without any reminder being necessary.

4. Suspension of the mission
In the event of non-payment of fee and cost invoices, the Lawyer reserves the right to suspend performance of the mission, of which he will inform the Client, drawing the Client's attention to the possible consequences.

5. Withdrawal of the mission
Should the Client wish to withdraw the mission from the Lawyer and transfer the file to another lawyer, the Client undertakes to pay without delay the fees, costs, disbursements and expenses due to the Lawyer for the diligence carried out prior to the withdrawal. If the withdrawal occurs after complete preparation of the file, the additional result fee will remain due to the Lawyer. The entire additional result fee will remain due to the Lawyer notwithstanding any withdrawal.

6. Disputes
Any dispute concerning the amount and the recovery of the Lawyer's fees, costs and disbursements may only be settled, failing agreement between the parties, by recourse to the procedure provided for in articles 174 et seq. of decree no. 91-1197 of 27 November 1991 organising the profession of lawyer. The President of the Paris Bar (Bâtonnier) is seised at the request of the more diligent party. It is expressly agreed between the parties that, in the event of a dispute, the amount of the fees, costs and disbursements calculated as provided in the Agreement and remaining due to the Lawyer must be deposited in the hands of the President of the Paris Bar, pending a final decision setting the fees, costs and disbursements.

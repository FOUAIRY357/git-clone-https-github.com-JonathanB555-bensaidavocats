1. Mission
The Client instructs the Lawyer to establish what assets situated in France the late Borislav Borozanov was entitled to receive, what became of them, and what action remains open to the Client in respect of them. The Client states that French inheritance tax was paid out of his father's estate in respect of assets situated in France which were never received, and that an apartment situated in Neuilly-sur-Seine was to devolve upon his father on the death of his aunt's husband.

More specifically, the Lawyer is to:
- obtain from the competent service de la publicité foncière the ownership history of the apartment situated in Neuilly-sur-Seine, and identify its present owner and the successive transfers of title
- search the fichier central des dispositions de dernières volontés in respect of each of the deceased
- identify the notary who administered the French estate, and obtain the declaration of estate, the property attestation and any draft deed of division
- carry out the FICOBA, AGIRA and FICOVIE searches in respect of the deceased
- establish whether French inheritance tax was paid in respect of assets never received, and on what basis
- deliver a written report setting out what was received, what was paid, where the assets stand today, and which courses of action remain open, on what legal basis and within what time limits

The Lawyer undertakes to carry out all due diligence and to implement all legal and procedural means to protect the Client's interests and to give them the best chances of success. The Lawyer will carry out all useful diligence in agreement with the Client. The Lawyer will keep the Client regularly informed of the progress of the mission entrusted to him.

2. Determination of the fee
Fixed monthly fee
For the performance of the mission, the fees are set on a fixed basis at the sum of EUR 3,500 excluding VAT per month (three thousand five hundred euros excluding tax per month). Amounts are stated excluding tax; VAT is due in addition, according to the regime applicable at the date of invoicing.
For information, the firm's usual hourly rate is set at EUR 600.00 excluding VAT, 2026 value.

These fees cover all diligence carried out within the scope of the mission: the searches described above, the correspondence with the land registry, with the notary and with the administration, and the drafting and finalisation of the written report.

The Agreement is entered into for an indefinite period and may be terminated by either party at the end of any month, by written notice given before the last day of that month. Fees already paid for a month begun remain acquired to the Lawyer.

At the end of the second month, the parties will review together the scope of the mission and the amount of the fee in the light of the results obtained.

The fee does not cover: any contentious or judicial proceedings, in France or elsewhere, which will be the subject of a separate agreement; the fees of local correspondents, in particular in Bulgaria and, should the file require it, in Greece; and disbursements, in particular the European Certificate of Succession, apostilles, sworn translations, land-registry charges and notarial charges, which are invoiced to the Client at cost.

Contingency (result) fee
In addition to the monthly fee above, a result fee of 10% excluding VAT will be due to the Lawyer, calculated on the sums actually recovered and on the value of the assets recovered in respect of the assets situated in France, whether by way of payment, of restitution of tax, of transfer of title, of set-off, or of an increase in the Client's share upon a division of an estate. Where an asset is recovered in kind, the basis of the result fee is its market value on the date on which the Client's title to it is established, as agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, as determined by an expert appointed jointly by them. Where the sums recovered are denominated in a currency other than the euro, they are converted at the European Central Bank reference rate applicable on the date of receipt.
This result fee will fall due upon the occurrence of the following trigger event: the effective receipt of the sums by the Client, or the establishment of the Client's title to the asset recovered. It will remain due to the Lawyer in full once that event occurs, including after the end or the interruption of the mission, and regardless of which lawyer finalised the file, notwithstanding any withdrawal of the mission or any succession of lawyers.

3. Payment
The monthly fee is payable in advance. The first month is payable upon signature of the Agreement, and each subsequent month on the first day of the month concerned. Invoices for costs and fees are payable on receipt. Failing payment when due, late-payment interest will be legally due, calculated at a rate equal to three times the legal interest rate as from the due date shown on the invoice, without any reminder being necessary.

4. Suspension of the mission
In the event of non-payment of fee and cost invoices, the Lawyer reserves the right to suspend performance of the mission, of which he will inform the Client, drawing the Client's attention to the possible consequences.

5. Withdrawal of the mission
Should the Client wish to withdraw the mission from the Lawyer and transfer the file to another lawyer, the Client undertakes to pay without delay the fees, costs, disbursements and expenses due to the Lawyer for the diligence carried out prior to the withdrawal. If the withdrawal occurs after complete preparation of the file, the additional result fee will remain due to the Lawyer. The entire additional result fee will remain due to the Lawyer notwithstanding any withdrawal.

6. Disputes
Any dispute concerning the amount and the recovery of the Lawyer's fees, costs and disbursements may only be settled, failing agreement between the parties, by recourse to the procedure provided for in articles 174 et seq. of decree no. 91-1197 of 27 November 1991 organising the profession of lawyer. The President of the Paris Bar (Bâtonnier) is seised at the request of the more diligent party. It is expressly agreed between the parties that, in the event of a dispute, the amount of the fees, costs and disbursements calculated as provided in the Agreement and remaining due to the Lawyer must be deposited in the hands of the President of the Paris Bar, pending a final decision setting the fees, costs and disbursements.

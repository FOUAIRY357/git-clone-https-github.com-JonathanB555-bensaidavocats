One point from our call is worth setting out now, because it bears on your timing.

The five-year period during which a new resident is taxed on French real estate only runs from the year in which the French domicile was established. A change of residence permit leaves that period running. So if your student years already counted, part of it has gone, and the year it ends is a figure you need before you commit to the talent passport.

Fixing that year is the object of our review. On what you have told us so far, your exposure may well prove narrower than you expect.

Two items would let us start.

- your United States income tax returns for the last three years, with their schedules
- your estimate of the days spent in France each year since your arrival

The rest of the list can follow at your convenience. A short call works just as well, if that is easier for you.

We remain at your disposal for any question.
